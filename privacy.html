2. Summary

Kalsett works without an account. Everything you create in the app – routines, checkpoints, settings, your history as well as photos and videos – is stored only locally on your device. We do not run any servers and we do not receive this data.

Kalsett contains no ads and no analytics or tracking services.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: for photos and videos when checking (only if you turn this on).
  • NFC: to read NFC tags.
  • Storage or photos and videos: to show and play recordings in the app and to save or open the backup file.
  • Internet: for purchases via Google Play and to open links.

You can revoke permissions at any time in the Android settings. The other features of the app remain available.

5. Purchases (Kalsett Premium)

Purchases and subscriptions are handled exclusively by Google Play. We do not receive any payment data. Google is responsible for this processing; the Google Privacy Policy applies. Google also processes data under its own responsibility when you download the app from Google Play.

6. Links to other providers

Kalsett contains links that open a website in your browser (e.g. “Discover NFC tags”, which leads to an online shop, and these legal pages). The respective provider is responsible for any data processing on those websites.

7. Android backup

Depending on your device settings, Android may include app data in your personal device backup (e.g. in your Google account). We have no access to it.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete checkpoints, routines, photos and videos in the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in the Android settings deletes all app data from your device. Backup files you saved yourself are deleted at the place where you stored them.

9. E-mails to us

If you write to us, we use your message and e-mail address only to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them when they are no longer needed and no legal retention periods apply.

10. Your rights

As we do not receive personal data from the app, your rights usually only concern messages you send us. Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

11. Children

Kalsett is not specifically directed at children under 13.

12. Changes

We update this privacy policy when the app changes. The current version is always available on this page.
